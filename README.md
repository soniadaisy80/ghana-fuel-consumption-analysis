# Fuel Consumption in Ghana: Economic and Environmental Analysis

This project investigates fuel consumption trends in Ghana and examines their relationship with key economic and environmental indicators, including GDP growth, inflation, and carbon dioxide (CO₂) emissions.
The analysis combines data visualization, statistical analysis, and business intelligence techniques to provide insights into how fuel usage influences economic performance and environmental sustainability.

Objectives
Analyze trends in fuel consumption across different fuel types.
Explore relationships between fuel consumption and GDP growth.
Examine the impact of fuel consumption on inflation.
Assess the connection between fuel use and CO₂ emissions.
Present findings through interactive and visual dashboards

Tools and Technologies
Microsoft Excel – Data cleaning and preparation
Python – Statistical analysis and data exploration
Pandas & NumPy – Data manipulation
Matplotlib & Seaborn – Data visualization
Power BI – Interactive dashboards and reporting
GitHub – Version control and project documentation

 Key Findings
Fuel consumption exhibits notable variation across the study period.
Petroleum-based fuels show a strong association with CO₂ emissions.
Certain fuel categories demonstrate a positive relationship with GDP growth.
Changes in fuel consumption may influence inflation through transportation and production costs.
Sustainable energy policies could help balance economic development with environmental protection.
